Dewby is a wearable ambient agent. It listens, with your consent, and turns your
conversations into clean, source-linked notes. When you say something worth acting
on, it does the follow-up: sourced research, drafts, calendar holds, work carried
out in a paired browser or on your Mac.

It lives on your iPhone and the Dewby Band, with a Mac menu bar app, a web
companion and a Chrome extension as its hands. Every note links back to the words
that produced it. Listening stays under your control. What Dewby remembers can be
read, and deleted.

Dewby is in early testing. [heydewby.com](https://heydewby.com)
